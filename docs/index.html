OotleGig

Escrowed micro-jobs with verifiable delivery receipts, on Tari Ootle.

SIMULATION — this page runs the exact rules of the OotleGig Rust template in your browser. No wallet, no network, no real funds. The on-chain version is the JobEscrow template in template/, proven by the engine tests (cargo test).

Every template rule checks the transaction signer. Switch roles to see the rejections a wrong signer gets.

3. Post a job (as the client)

4. Work & deliver (as the worker)

5. Release

Approval pays the worker and mints the delivery receipt. If the client never approves, the worker claims the same payout once the review window has passed. Either way the receipt can be collected exactly once.

Log

    OotleGig is an entry for the Tari November Build Contest 2026 (theme: MVP), by Indra Dwi Junianto. MIT licensed. The template has not been published to Esmeralda or mainnet yet; this simulation and the engine tests are the evidence. Disputes are out of scope for v1: a client who dislikes a delivery simply does not approve it (the worker's protection is the delivery record plus the timeout claim only when the client goes silent).
